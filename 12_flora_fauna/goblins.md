# Goblins

Goblins are made, not born.  You throw a goblin ear in a barrel with some other gross stuff (fish guts, rotten eggs, sour milk) and seal it up for a week, then a full grown is spawned from the ear.  It only takes one ear to make a new goblin, so they will decapitate their fallen brothers and resulting in two more years goblins spawned from each of their dead.

Goblins living just below the surface and burrowing into sewers and people's root cellars for the necessary breeding ingredients. Virtually all live below ground or in shallow caves.  The majority are part of the subterranean goblin nation of Khoshinvak (coloquially called 'Kosh') rules by the Goblin Queen.  Her true name is only known to the inner goblin court.
